[The compact record](v05-network-review.json) retains actual outcomes and audited source identities. The final packaged test suite carries these controls forward.

## Protocol integrity and holdout behavior

A separate review challenged completed-sample omission, candidate selection, support overlap, thresholds, holdout exposure, CSV coherence, replay claims and interruption. It found that a completed sample could be relabelled unrun, a changed selection was not checked against the original fit receipt, identical controls could occupy both splits, CSV could contradict verified JSON, replay could be relabelled first confirmation, and unstarted holdout cases were absent from the partial attempt ledger.

Targeted closure verifies rejection of each reproduced defect, including a replay attack with regenerated HTML. It confirms zero holdout observation opens/stats during fitting, legitimate budget-limited selection with the full denominator, threshold preservation, and exposure durability before observation reading. [The compact protocol record](v05-protocol-review.json) retains actual outcomes. The final source/package suite is separate evidence.

## Report review

The original network report was observed at desktop, phone, 320-pixel narrow width and CSS zoom/reflow proxies. Review found ambiguous custody wording, incomparable chart scales, incomplete artifact navigation and small chart labels. Corrections split stored/unknown quantities, share chart scales, link every artifact, state numerical error/steps/bounds and enlarge chart text.

The reviewed export followed every artifact link and preserved bytes. Final candidate browser evidence must still identify its exact report/source digest. Browser checks include keyboard focus, table scrolling, disclosures, contained reflow and offline resources. CSS zoom proxies do not establish actual browser zoom or universal accessibility; no screen-reader or human observation is inferred.

## Limits

These reviews are targeted rather than exhaustive. They do not prove every admitted graph, arbitrary hostile operating-system behavior, power-loss durability, rights to external data, statistical independence, global identifiability or any real-world benefit. Hashes preserve evidence identity and consistency; they cannot authenticate an author who controls all files and receipts. Historical reviews and failed probes remain retained separately from product examples.
